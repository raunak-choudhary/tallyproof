This is a short explainer on data pipelines, and how Tallyproof uses them.
A data pipeline is a chain of steps that moves data from where it is produced to where it is used, cleaning and reshaping it along the way. Batch pipelines run on a schedule over large files. Streaming pipelines handle events one at a time, as they arrive. Tallyproof has both.
The batch pipeline starts with public bulk files from USAspending: about twenty-seven gigabytes of CSV for two fiscal years, each archive verified against its checksum. Spark reads them one member at a time, types every column, removes duplicates, and drops anything personal: executive names and pay, contact details, and awards to individual people. The result is a typed Parquet table, the silver layer.
Before anything moves on, Deequ checks eleven constraints on all thirteen million rows: complete and unique keys, dates inside the fiscal years, amounts within bounds. If one fails, the build stops.
The gold step turns clean rows into a million ledger events in arrival order, and mixes in a seeded set of labeled anomalies, so the right answers are known before any model is asked. A replay tool then publishes those events into Kafka at a controlled pace.
From there the streaming pipeline takes over. Ingestion validates each event and appends it to the ledger exactly once, and the checkpointer signs the log. Projections derive the journal and reconciliation breaks and push them live. The same stream also lands in an Iceberg lake, where DuckDB re-runs the reconciliation rules as SQL, and the two results are compared break for break.
Why build it this way? Because real data is messy. Report dates turned out to be timestamps. A time zone bug shifted every event by four hours. And a million events don't fit in memory on an eight gigabyte laptop, which forced a streaming design that keeps memory flat at any size. Splitting the lake from the ledger is the usual divide between analytics and transactions, and the pipeline keeps the two in agreement.
In the repository, the batch pipeline is the tallyproof pipeline package: silver, quality, gold, overlay, replay, and lake. The streaming side is the ingestion app, the ledger, and the projections.
