Why Kafka at all? It decouples the producers from the single-writer ledger, buffers bursts, and makes every run replayable. One lesson from building it: derived views used to consume from Kafka too. They now read the ledger directly, in leaf order, because that is the signed source of truth, and it gives exactly-once output for free. Kafka carries what leaves the database: appended events, breaks, and checkpoints, for the live stream and outside consumers.
